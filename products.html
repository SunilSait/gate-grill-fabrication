Stainless Steel

SS Railings & Balustrades

Sleek, modern stainless steel railings that combine safety with sophisticated aesthetics. Choose from tubular SS, cable-wire, glass-insert, and design-panel options for balconies, terraces, and staircases.

We use ISI-marked SS 304 (inland) and SS 316 (coastal/high-humidity) grade steel. All welds are TIG-welded for clean, strong joints. Mirror or brushed finish available.

Aluminum Decorative Grills

Aluminum grills offer the perfect balance of aesthetics, durability, and value. Being naturally rust-proof and lightweight, they're ideal for windows, ventilation openings, and decorative facade panels.
