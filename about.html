From the initial laser measurement at your doorstep to master fabrication, multi-layer rust-proofing, and white-glove installation.

Precision Engineering

Bridging Architecture & Industrial Execution

Before a single bar of steel is cut, our structural design studio creates millimetric CAD blueprints tailored to your villa's specific pillars, driveway slopes, and exterior aesthetic. We test wind load resistance, pivot weight dynamics, and automated swing clearance.

0.1mm Cutting Tolerance
Interactive 3D Preview
Step 01

Free Site Visit & 3D Laser Measurement

Our senior structural surveyor visits your site with optical laser tools to evaluate driveway slopes, pillar plumbness, road clearance, and exact millimeter span.

✓ 100% Free • No Obligation
Step 02

3D CAD Modeling & Transparent BOQ

We convert your selected design into a millimetric 3D CAD render. You receive an itemized Bill of Quantities stating exact steel gauge, weight, and brand-certified materials.

✓ Zero Hidden Costs
Step 03

Fiber Laser CNC Pattern & Jali Cutting

Intricate jali work, modern geometric accents, and compound sheet panels are precision-cut on high-wattage fiber lasers ensuring razor-sharp edges steel.

✓ 0.1mm Tolerances
Step 04

Master Welding & 7-Tank Rust Immersion

Pieces are joined with continuous TIG/MIG shield arcs, followed by 7-tank chemical degreasing, zinc phosphating, and oven-cured electrostatic powder coating.

✓ 500+ Hr Salt Spray Tested
Step 05

On-Site Installation & Motor Automation

Our master rigging crew installs heavy-duty ball-bearing hinges, anti-lift brackets, vibration dampeners, and optional Italian-made smart automated sliding or swing gate motors.

✓ Silent • Smooth Rollers
Step 06

25-Point Inspection & 10-Yr Warranty

A certified quality engineer executes a 25-point safety checklist verifying balance, plumb alignment, coating thickness, and safety sensors. Handover includes official warranty.

✓ Official Warranty Card

Timeline Chart

Our Journey: From Artisanal Forge to Industry Leader

A chronological record of milestones, technological leaps, and over 5,200 successful installations across South India.

2008

The Humble Forge

IronCraft is founded with a 900 sq.ft workshop and 3 master blacksmiths in Chennai, dedicated to custom wrought iron driveway gates and handcrafted balcony grills.

Initial team of 3 artisans • 45 gates fabricated
2012

Stainless Steel & Toughened Glass

Introduced Jindal Grade 304/316 architectural stainless steel balustrades and frameless glass staircase railings, catering to the surge in contemporary luxury villas.

Crossed 500+ residential installations
2016

15,000 Sq.Ft Modern Fabrication Plant

Moved operations to a heavy-duty industrial facility equipped with overhead bridge cranes, computerized sheet benders, and high-purity argon gas TIG welding stations.

Tripled production capacity to 40 gates/month
2019

CNC Fiber Laser & 7-Tank Powder Coating

Invested in 6kW high-speed fiber-laser cutters for intricate architectural jali gate designs and an automated 7-tank chemical pre-treatment plant ensuring 15-year rust immunity.

ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Certified
2022

Smart IoT Automated Gates & Compound Walls

Partnered with European gate automation manufacturers to offer high-torque smartphone-controlled sliding/swing motors, obstacle sensors, and modular metal compound walls.

Over 1,200 automated gate installations across Tamil Nadu
2024 – Present

5,200+ Milestone & Digital 3D AR Previews

Surpassed 5,200 installations across Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, and Bengaluru. Launched instant on-site Augmented Reality (AR) gate previews for homeowners and architects.
