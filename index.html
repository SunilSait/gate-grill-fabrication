Materials

The Right Material for Every Need

We work with three primary materials — each with distinct advantages. Our experts help you choose the best one for your budget, aesthetics, and location.

Mild Steel (MS)

Most affordable, highly workable. Ideal for heavy-duty gates with powder coat protection.

Stainless Steel (SS)

Premium corrosion-resistant material. Perfect for coastal areas and modern aesthetics.

Aluminum

Lightweight, naturally rust-free, zero maintenance. Perfect for modern CNC grills and gates.
